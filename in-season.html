Advanced weekly metrics
Target share, air yards share, WOPR, and yards per route run (YPRR), sourced from nflverse's public data. Nothing polls automatically — click "Run aggregation" (e.g. once a Monday/Tuesday after games finalize) and it pulls in whatever weeks aren't cached yet, catching you up in one go if you missed a week. Routes run is an approximation (on-field participation during charted dropback plays for WR/TE/RB) — there's no public feed of true team route charting, so treat YPRR as directionally right, not exact.
